1.  BS in Artificial Intelligence Upper-Division Electives You need 64 upper-division units total for the major: 20 units of core courses and 44 units of elective courses (typically 11 four-unit courses). The 44 elective units break down into: 4 units of Ethics: CSE 194, PHIL 174, or POLI 121D. 12 units of AI Electives: Chosen from an approved list across CSE, DSC, COGS, MATH, ECE, and PHIL. 28 units of CSE Electives: Must include at least 8 units tagged Systems, at least 8 units tagged Theory/Abstraction, and at least 8 units tagged Applications of Computing.

2.  Warren College Writing Requirements. First-year students: After satisfying the UC Entry Level Writing Requirement (ELWR), you must complete WCWP 10A and WCWP 10B at UCSD for a letter grade. Immediately after WCWP 10A-B, first-year students also take a two-course Ethics and Society sequence (PHIL 27 or POLI 27, followed by PHIL 28 or POLI 28), which continues writing instruction. Transfer students: Transfers who have completed lower-division GE/writing requirements (such as IGETC or a transfer agreement) take WCWP 100 (Academic Writing) at UCSD for a letter grade instead of WCWP 10A-B.

3.  Revelle College Humanities Sequence. Revelle requires a five-course interdisciplinary humanities sequence (HUM 1 through HUM 5) spanning history, philosophy, and literature, which fulfills both the humanities and writing requirements. You must satisfy ELWR before enrolling. HUM 1 and HUM 2 (6 units each) provide intensive writing instruction and must be taken before HUM 3, HUM 4, and HUM 5 (4 units each). Transfer students entering Fall 2024 or later must complete one upper-division humanities course (HUM 100).

4.  Sixth College General Education Requirements. For first-year students, Sixth College GE requirements include: Culture, Art, and Technology (CAT) Core Sequence: CAT 1 (4 units), CAT 2 (6 units), and CAT 3 (6 units) taken in order after satisfying ELWR. (The UCSD catalog GE chart also notes CAT 125, a 4-unit upper-division writing course for public audiences; check your degree audit for admit-year applicability.) Lower-Division Breadth (11 courses): Information Technology Fluency (1 computing/programming course), Social Analysis (2 social science courses), Narrative, Aesthetic, and Historical Reasoning (2 humanities courses), Analytical and Scientific Methods (2 science courses), Structured Reasoning (1 math or logic course), Exploring Data (1 statistics course), and Art Making (8 units). Upper-Division Experiential Learning Requirement: One 4-unit upper-division course involving hands-on experiential learning (such as community engagement, an internship, research/design, art/performance, or study abroad). (Transfer students have modified GE requirements depending on their transfer/IGETC certification.)

5.  Required Courses for the Bioengineering Major (B.S. in Bioengineering). For the general B.S. in Bioengineering (separate from the Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, and BioSystems tracks), the required major courses are: Math: MATH 18, 20A, 20B, 20C, 20D, 20E. Science: BILD 1; CHEM 6A, 6B, 7L; PHYS 2A, 2B, 2BL, 2C, 2CL. Lower-Division Engineering: BENG 1; MAE 3, MAE 8, MAE 40. Upper-Division Core: BENG 100, 103B, 110, 112A, 112B, 122A, 125, 130, 140A, 140B, 152, 172, 186A, 186B, 187A-D (senior design), 188A-B; MAE 107, MAE 150. Electives: Two department-approved technical electives (plus college GE/HSS courses).

6.  What CSE 150A Covers. CSE 150A (Introduction to Artificial Intelligence: Probabilistic Reasoning and Decision-Making, 4 units) covers probabilistic models used in modern AI. Specific topics include probabilistic methods for reasoning and decision-making under uncertainty, inference and learning in Bayesian networks, and prediction and planning in Markov decision processes, along with applications in intelligent systems, speech and natural language processing, information retrieval, and robotics.

7.  Units Needed to Graduate from UCSD. To graduate with a BA or BS from UCSD, you need a minimum of 180 quarter units, including at least 60 upper-division units (along with completing your major, college GE, university requirements such as ELWR/AHI/DEI, senior residency, and a minimum 2.0 UC GPA).

8.  Math Courses Needed for Mechanical Engineering. Mechanical Engineering majors must complete MATH 18 (Linear Algebra) and MATH 20A, 20B, 20C, 20D, and 20E. (The major also requires MAE 107, Computational Methods, in the upper-division core.)

9.  What to Take Next as an AI Major After CSE 12 and 21. Assuming you have finished CSE 8B or 11 and CSE 20 (the prerequisites for CSE 12 and 21), your next steps toward the B.S. in AI lower-division and early upper-division requirements are: CSE 15L or CSE 29 (Systems Programming and Software Tools) if you haven't taken it yet, followed by CSE 30. Once you have CSE 12, CSE 21, and CSE 15L or CSE 29, you will meet the prerequisites for CSE 100 (Advanced Data Structures). CSE 25 (Introduction to AI), which only requires an introductory programming class such as CSE 8B or CSE 11. Math & Probability/Stats: Progress through MATH 20A-C (or 31BH) and MATH 18 (or 31AH), plus your Probability and Statistics requirement (one of MATH 181A, MATH 183, ECE 109, ECON 120A, or CSE 103). Finishing MATH 18, MATH 20C, CSE 25, and CSE 15L or 29 unlocks CSE 55, and finishing Probability/Stats, MATH 18, MATH 20A, CSE 12, and CSE 15L or 29 unlocks CSE 150A. Other Lower-Division: Complete your 4-unit General Science requirement and 4-unit lower-division CSE elective if not yet completed.

10. Requirements for the Bioinformatics Major. UCSD offers Bioinformatics through three departments, all sharing a core foundation while adding department-specific courses: Shared Core: Calculus and linear algebra (MATH 18, 20A-C), physics (PHYS 2A-C), CSE 11 (or 8A-B), CSE 12, CSE 21, CSE 100, CSE 101, genetics (BICD 100), molecular biology (BIMM 100), biochemistry, MATH 186, and the bioinformatics core series (CSE/BIMM/BENG 181, CSE/BIMM/BENG 182, BENG 183, and CSE 185). CSE (BS in Computer Science with Specialization in Bioinformatics): 128 major units total, emphasizing CS core/electives alongside BIBC 100 or CHEM 114A or BIBC 102, BICD 100, BIMM 100, BIMM 101, and the 20-unit bioinformatics series. Bioengineering (BS in Bioengineering: Bioinformatics): Adds engineering math/courses (such as MATH 20D-E, BENG 1, 100, 120, 125, 130, 168), senior design/bioengineering sequences (BENG 187A-D, 188A-B), and 8 units of engineering technical electives. Biological Sciences (BS in Biology with Specialization in Bioinformatics): Requires 48 upper-division units, including lower-division BILD 1, 3, 4, 5, CHEM 6A-B, CHEM 40A-B, CSE 11 (or 8A-B), 12, 21, MATH 18, 20A-C, PHYS 2A-C; upper-division CSE 100, 101, biochemistry (BIBC 100/102 or CHEM 114A/B), BICD 100, BIMM 100, BIMM 181, BIMM 182, BENG 183, CSE 185, MATH 186, and at least 8 units of upper-division Biology electives.

11. Prerequisites for PHYS 2A. The catalog prerequisites for PHYS 2A are MATH 10A-B, or MATH 20A, 20B, 20C, or 31BH (with MATH 20A or placement into MATH 20B or higher serving as the standard entry point). Recommended preparation is prior or concurrent enrollment in MATH 20B.

Because degree requirements and course prerequisites can vary by your admit year and college, please confirm your specific plan with your academic advisor via the Virtual Advising Center (VAC).
